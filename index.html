Proved scope

The paper analyzes a fixed finite-dimensional single reflection-pair contribution as the pair approaches the critical line. It separates exact negative detection from vanishing geometric robustness and classifies higher-order real-symmetric transverse jets.

Collision expansion

W_delta - W_0 = O(delta^2), with an explicit quadratic coefficient.

Exact vs robust

A discontinuous negative-presence detector can remain exact while the geometric PSD-distance margin tends to zero.

Higher-order jets

lambda_-(delta) = -2m||w||^2 delta^(4q+2) + ..., giving leading orders 2, 6, 10, 14, ... within the real-symmetric analytic class.

Scope: no RH proof; no general aggregate interacting-pair theorem; no infinite-dimensional conclusion; no claim that every higher-order branch is realized by the actual taper family.
